# Wall elevation positions surveyed technical points

Survey records existing technical points on the room-side face of a wall using models from the skAItch technical catalogue. Placing or dragging a point in the wall elevation validates its position for reconstruction. The user can refine either axis with a measured cote from either wall edge or from the finished floor. A dragged axis is estimated and an unchanged axis keeps its measurement.

A point placed within the wall bounds can be reconstructed from its stored position, including points in earlier surveys. An estimated horizontal position follows the proportions of the drawn face, while a measured cote retains its distance from the chosen edge. Survey flags points outside their wall and a previously reconstructed point keeps its last model position until corrected.

Survey uses one surface selection for both photos and the wall elevation. For the selected wall face, the user can switch between the photos and the elevation in the same screen. Photos provide a visual reference, and technical points are placed and measured in the elevation.

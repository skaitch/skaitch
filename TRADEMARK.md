# Trademark policy

The source code of skAItch is free software under the GNU AGPL-3.0 (see `LICENSE`).
The licence covers the code. It does **not** grant any right to the name
**skAItch**, its logo or its visual identity ("the Marks").

## What you may do without asking

- Use, build, modify and run skAItch, including under its name, for yourself or
  inside your organisation.
- Refer to the project by name to describe it truthfully ("works with skAItch",
  "based on skAItch", articles, tutorials, talks).
- Redistribute **unmodified** official releases under the skAItch name.

## What requires a different name

If you distribute a **modified** version, or offer it as a hosted service, you
must:

- give it a different name and logo that cannot be confused with skAItch;
- not suggest that it is official or endorsed by the skAItch project;
- keep the AGPL notices and you may state that it is "based on skAItch".

## What is not allowed

- Selling or hosting a product under the skAItch name without written permission.
- Using the Marks in a domain name, company name or app store listing in a way
  that suggests an official affiliation.
